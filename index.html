Great Glitter Storm Grounds Tooth Fairy Fleet

Thousands of tooth collections delayed as wild winds and sparkle-hail sweep across ToothTown. Flights resume tonight.

By Pip Moonwhistle, Chief Weather Correspondent · Updated this morning

A fairy shelters from sparkle-hail outside the Molar Street Depot. Photo: ToothTown Tribune

A powerful storm swept through ToothTown yesterday, bringing gale-force winds, thunder and dangerous "sparkle-hail" that forced the Royal Tooth Fairy Guild to ground its entire flying fleet for the night.

Fairy wings are delicate, and Guild safety rules forbid flying when winds reach more than 40 wand-lengths per second. By early evening, gusts over the Enamel Mountains were nearly double that.

"We had fairies all packed and ready with their coin pouches," said one depot manager. "But you simply can't fly a fairy through a storm like that. Safety first — always."

"We know how exciting it is to lose a tooth, and we are so sorry to every child who had to wait. Please keep your tooth safe under your pillow. Our fairies will be out tonight, and nobody will be missed." — Queen Opaline Brightwing, Head of the Royal Tooth Fairy Guild

Meteorologists at the ToothTown Weather Tower say the storm moved out to sea early this morning, leaving clear skies and gentle breezes — perfect flying weather. Extra fairies have been called in from the Wisdom Valley and Baby Tooth Bay depots to help clear the backlog.

The Guild has asked families to leave teeth exactly where they are. Fairies keep careful lists, and every delayed collection will be completed tonight. Some fairies have even hinted that children who waited so patiently may find a little extra sparkle for their trouble.
